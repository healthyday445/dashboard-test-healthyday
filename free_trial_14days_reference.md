# 🆓 Free Trial (14-Day Batch) — Dashboard Structure & Reference

This document provides a complete guide to the **14-Days Free Trial / Ongoing Batch** dashboard structure, regular session slots, special bonus sessions, and the day-by-day experience for users.

---

## 📅 1. Regular Live Yoga Sessions

Free trial users access daily live sessions on YouTube. There are **no recordings available for free trial users**.

### ⏰ Daily Timings (IST)
The dashboard displays the regular session card according to the time of day:

| Slot Type | Session Times | Dashboard View & Next Slots Label |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning Slots** | `5:30 AM`, `6:30 AM`, `7:30 AM`, `8:30 AM` | Active from **5:00 AM** to **9:30 AM**. Shows "LIVE" badge during these slots. |
| **Evening Slots** | `4:30 PM`, `5:30 PM`, `6:30 PM` | Active from **4:00 PM** to **7:30 PM**. Shows "LIVE" badge during these slots. |
| **Off-Hours / No Sessions** | Any other time | Shows **No Sessions Right Now** card. Next slots list is shown dynamically. |

## 🗺️ 3. Day-by-Day Dashboard Structure Map

The dashboard changes layout and unlocks features day-by-day.

### 🟢 Week 1: Days 1 to 7 (Onboarding & Referral Focus)
During Week 1, the primary focus is attendance habits and referral sharing to win gifts.

* **Day 1**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Standard Week 1 Dashboard.
  * **Attendance Grid:** Day 1 shows as **Yellow** (missed) or **Green** (if user clicks join). Days 2-14 show as **Future** (semi-transparent).
  * **Referral Gifts Milestone Card:** Visible. Shows progress towards 5 referrals milestone.
  * **Refer & Win Card:** Visible. Includes copy link and WhatsApp share.
  * **Referral Popup Overlay:** Shows automatically on dashboard load to invite users to refer (can be dismissed).

* **Day 2**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Standard Week 1 Dashboard.
  * **Attendance Grid:** Day 1 (Green/Yellow depending on attendance), Day 2 active, Days 3-14 Future.
  * **Referral Popup Overlay:** Appears if dismissed yesterday (displays once per day if not permanently dismissed).

* **Day 3 (Face Yoga Bonus Day)**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Special Bonus Session view during active window (**8:00 PM – 9:00 PM**).
  * **Live Window (8:30 PM – 9:00 PM):** Displays Face Yoga live session banner with red "LIVE" badge and "JOIN NOW" button.
  * **Outside Active Window:** Shows standard trial dashboard.

* **Day 4**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Standard Week 1 Dashboard.
  * **Attendance Grid:** Days 1-3 display historic results, Day 4 active, Days 5-14 Future.

* **Day 5 (Meditation Bonus Day)**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Special Bonus Session view active (**7:30 PM – 8:30 PM**).
  * **Live Window (8:00 PM – 8:30 PM):** Meditation card shows "LIVE" badge and directs to the Meditation class link.

* **Day 6**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Standard Week 1 Dashboard.

* **Day 7 (Weight Loss Bonus Day)**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Special Bonus Session view active (**10:00 AM – 11:00 AM**).
  * **Live Window (10:30 AM – 11:00 AM):** Weight Loss card shows "LIVE" badge.
  * **End of Week 1:** Last day for Week 1 Referral Milestones card.

---

### 🟡 Week 2: Days 8 to 14 (Conversion & Subscription Focus)
During Week 2, the Referral Milestone card is hidden, and the Pricing and Comparison section is displayed to encourage users to purchase a paid subscription before their trial ends.

* **Day 8**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Week 2 Dashboard.
  * **Milestones Card:** ❌ Hidden.
  * **Pricing & Comparison Section:**  Visible. Shows plan cards (1, 3, 6, 12 Months) with a countdown of `7 days left`.
  * **Attendance Grid:** Displays Days 1-7 historic blocks, Day 8 active, Days 9-14 Future.

* **Day 9**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Week 2 Dashboard.
  * **Pricing & Comparison Section:** Visible with `6 days left`.

* **Day 10 (Breath Work Bonus Day)**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Special Bonus Session view active (**8:00 PM – 9:00 PM**).
  * **Live Window (8:30 PM – 9:00 PM):** Breath Work session card shows "LIVE" badge.
  * **Other times:** Standard Week 2 Dashboard with pricing active.

* **Day 11**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Week 2 Dashboard with pricing active (`4 days left`).

* **Day 12**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Week 2 Dashboard with pricing active (`3 days left`).

* **Day 13**
  * **Dashboard Layout:** Week 2 Dashboard with pricing active (`2 days left`).

* **Day 14 (Sleep Bonus Day & Final Day)**
  * **Dashboard Layout:**
    * **10:00 AM – 11:00 AM:** Special Sleep Session bonus card is active.
    * **Other times:** Standard Week 2 Dashboard with pricing active (`1 day left`).
  * **Day 14 Attendance Box:** Becomes active.
  * **End of Trial:** At midnight, the status shifts to `elapsed` / trial completed.

---

### 🔴 Day 15+ (Trial Completed / Ended Status)
Once the 14 days are elapsed, the trial access is closed.
* **Dashboard Layout:** **Trial Completed / Ended Dashboard**.
* **Pill Badge:** Shows "TRIAL ENDED" (Red badge).
* **Header Message:** *"Your 14-Days FREE Classes are completed"*.
* **Live Classes Join:** ❌ Disabled.
* **Pricing Section:** Prominently featured to prompt subscription.
* **Refer & Win Card:** Visible below pricing to allow continued sharing.

## 🛠️ 4. Attendance Day Box Status Logic

The status of each day (1 to 14) in the attendance grid is computed as follows:

```typescript
const dayStatus = Array.from({ length: 14 }, (_, i) => {
  const dayNum = i + 1;
  
  // 1. Future Days: If the box day is greater than current batch day
  if (dayNum > currentDay) return "future";
  
  // 2. Attended Days (Green):
  // Checks if the user clicked join today (stored in joinedDays local state)
  // OR if the date string (YYYY-MM-DD) is in the student's database attendance tracker
  const didJoin = joinedDays.includes(dayNum);
  const d = new Date(batchOrigin);
  d.setDate(batchOrigin.getDate() + i);
  const dateStr = `${d.getFullYear()}-${String(d.getMonth() + 1).padStart(2, "0")}-${String(d.getDate()).padStart(2, "0")}`;
  
  if (didJoin || attendedDates.has(dateStr)) return "green";
  
  // 3. Missed Days (Yellow):
  // Past or current days that do not have any join or attendance records
  return "yellow";
});
```
